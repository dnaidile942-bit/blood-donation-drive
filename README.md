# blood-donation-drive

**PROBLEM STATEMENT** : 
     NSS needs simple way to run blood donation drive without paper registration .
     In app students are able to register as donors and book the slot and cancel with roll no , weight and blood group . 
     Day of donating they should record each appointment as donated . 
     They want to see how many litres of blood collected for each group.
     Call the particular blood group donors who have ability to donate  . 
     
rules for drive -> donor must wait 90 days of donation 
                   1 donor can book 1 slot 
                   they cant book more than 10 people per slot 
                   reject the invalid input (unknown blood group , underweight , past dates 
                   , double roll no )with clear error message .
                   
We have to build backend with 9 endpoints , frontend and it should show API's error message.
